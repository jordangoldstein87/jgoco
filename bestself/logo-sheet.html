The same logo, drawn properly.

Not a new mark. The figure, the lotus, the colours and the order are all hers. What changed is the drawing and the type.

What she has today

Seven petals hand-drawn at different widths, a sketched double waterline, and a wordmark with uneven tracking. It holds up on a desktop header and falls apart below about 40 pixels.

Cleaned up

One petal shape, evenly spaced and evenly scaled. One clean arc for the water. Poppins, her own font, with the tracking fixed, and a real italic serif for her name.

On dark

The wordmark flips to white. The lotus and the figure do not change, so it stays the same logo on a dark header, a footer or a sign.

It survives being small

The part the current one struggles with

A Google Business Profile avatar, a browser tab and a text message thumbnail are all under 50 pixels. The mark has to work there, because that is where most people meet it first.

What actually changed

  • 1Seven different petal outlines became one shape, placed at even angles and even scales.
  • 2The colour run is regular now, violet through teal to magenta, with her own brand teal at the centre.
  • 3The figure is built from four strokes on a single axis, so it reads as a person at any size.
  • 4The sketched double waterline became one arc.
  • 5The wordmark is Poppins, which is already the font on her website, with the letter spacing corrected.
  • 6Her name is set in a real italic serif instead of a slanted sans.
