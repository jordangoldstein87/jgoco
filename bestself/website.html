Meet your physician

Dr. Cheryl Harris Geer, D.O., F.A.C.O.G.

A board-certified obstetrician-gynecologist who has cared for women in Ventura County since 1999, and a Sciton Luminary who teaches other physicians how to use these lasers well. She founded the Center for Women's Well Being in Camarillo in 2001 and Best Self Medical Arts in Westlake Village in 2017.

Her approach comes from a simple place: the things that change how you feel, your hormones, your energy, your skin, are connected. Treating them as one picture is why patients who have been told it is just their age find something different here.
